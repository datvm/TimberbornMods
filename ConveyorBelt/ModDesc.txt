Conveyor Belt v2 is here. This is a full revamp of the old mod. Lay a network as complex as you want: straights, corners, and risers, under platforms and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3801726769]Pipes[/url].

[img]https://files.catbox.moe/6mqj8p.gif[/img]

[b]Not compatible with the previous Conveyor Belt.[/b] That is why this is a separate Workshop item. Finish your current save on the old mod, then start a new save with this one. An old save loaded here will need its belt network rebuilt from scratch.

[h1]New Belts[/h1]
Belts now have 4 speeds: a cheap Log belt moves 1 item/hour and is available from the start. Unlock the later belts as you go, up to 100 items/hour. Each speed draws a different amount of power. A Riser Up draws double. A Riser Down draws half. All of these buildings are in their own Conveyor Belt group, next to Storage.
Horizontal Belts only occupy the bottom of the tile. You can build other buildings on the same tile, including [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3801726769]Pipes[/url] or Platforms.
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/18373699010988517383/78FE1D0A3824338A30E3CFBBC78E5E3440819836/?imw=5000&imh=5000[/img]

Carrying pieces: Belt (straight), Corner, Riser Up, and Riser Down. Speed 4 also has the Impermeable Belt. It is waterproof and blocks water (see floodable belts below). These pieces can always transfer goods in and out of a building.

Junction pieces are still here and are better: Merger, Splitter and Smart Splitter. Lift In and Out are vertical variants of Merger and Splitter. Smart Splitter can now assign a port to Overflow as well.

[h1]Important notes:[/h1]
[list]
[*] Junction ports only work with plain belts. Do not chain a Merger or Splitter into another Merger or Splitter. Goods will not move. This includes Lift In and Lift Out: do not put a Merger or Splitter on the output of a Lift Out.
[*] Belts are floodable. They [b]keep working[/b], but the goods on them are washed away. The Impermeable Belt is the only exception.
[*] Warning when stuck is now opt-in: select an important belt you want to be warned about when goods are stuck on it, and check "Warn when stuck".
[*] Belts cannot be partially powered. If you do not provide enough power to the network, the belts will stop working.
[/list]

[h1]Mod Settings[/h1]
You can change some settings for the mod: Main Menu or in-game menu -> Mods -> Settings button next to the mod's name.
- Animation: You can turn off animations and good boxes on the belts to increase performance.
- Always show Arrows: always show the direction arrows on the belts even when you do not select one. There is also an option to hide them all the time when you select a belt.

[h1]Modded Factions[/h1]
The belts can be added to modded factions by subscribing to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815025887]Modded Factions Building Support[/url]. For EP, you may need to subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3591896694]Treated Planks For Emberpelts[/url]. It is not tested for modded factions, however, and support is considered on a case-by-case basis.

This version also uses a new simulation, if you see any issue, please report it in the comment or on Discord.
