Can your beavers launch a 90kg projectile over 300 meters? Now they can! This mod adds Crane Heads you place on top of a Crane. Each finished head changes how that Crane works.

Heads live in the [b]Crane Heads[/b] group under District Management. Place the center on top of a Crane Section. You can only put one Crane Head on top of a Crane, so choose wisely! (But you can destroy and replace it with another head if needed.)

[h1]Jib[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10379442352424378860/FF2120F4422F478C151ED80B547880238DA0FAB0/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false[/img]

Extend the working area by 1 (3x3 to 5x5). Select the Crane to see the working-area overlay.

[h1]Trebuchet[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/13132077091183884715/00AC79ECA2A0EACCF0CD68124736C017D43CCCDC/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false[/img]

- Range: 1 tile per Crane Section (the Crane base does not count).
- Weight limit: 90. Overweight payloads will not launch or accept more goods until you lighten them.
- Launch cost: 2 Planks per shot (the Crane stocks up to 3 shots).
- Cooldown: 1 hour.

On the Trebuchet panel: add goods, click [b]Choose target[/b], then [b]Fire[/b] (one shot) or tick [b]Repeat[/b]. The Crane worker fills the payload and launch cost.
The shot must have a clear path (like a zipline cable) and land on unoccupied ground or the top of a solid building. The arc peak is the Trebuchet's height plus the section count, so a short shot can land higher than the head.

[h1]Cranetop Windmill[/h1]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12269331650364366019/52C55E7E17ECE941C05F5C41377EB8551B1A2793/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false[/img]

Power shafts connect at the Crane base. Output: 300 hp scaled by wind, plus [b]+10% per Crane Section[/b] (the Crane base does not count). Below 30% wind, or with no mill attached, output is 0.

[h1]FAQS[/h1]
Q: The head will not place?
A: It must sit on a finished Crane Section. Use [b]Build higher[/b] on the Crane first, then place the head on the top section.

Q: Can I stack another section on a head?
A: No. Finish the mast height you want, then put the head on top.

Q: How far can the Trebuchet throw?
A: Horizontal range equals the number of Crane Sections. A 10-section mast reaches 10 tiles. The landing tile cannot be the Trebuchet's own tile.

Q: Who carries the payload?
A: The Crane worker. Haulers are not required, but they can still stock the Crane so the worker stays on the mast.
