This mod adds all Iron Teeth buildings and plants to Folktails, along with their goods and needs.
[b]Important:[/b] breeding in Iron Teeth housing needs [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3446296053]Configurable Housing[/url]. See below.

[h1]Why?[/h1]
[b]Q: Why modify Folktails instead of making a new faction?[/b]
A: You can keep playing an existing Folktails save and get every Iron Teeth stuff. That is the same practical benefit as [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3656686888]Configurable Faction[/url] without extra setup.
If you don't want it anymore, disable this mod and you are back on normal Folktails though buildings that vanilla Folktails should not have will be gone from that save.
[b]Most importantly:[/b] other modded buildings (like Ladder) that already support Folktails keep working.

[b]Q: Why not just use Configurable Faction?[/b]
A: Configurable Faction is the full mixer: pick every building, plant, good, and need yourself. This mod is the ready-made plate.
[b]Do not use this together with Configurable Faction.[/b]

[b]Q: Why not just use Greedy / other mods?[/b]
A: I am not aware of any Greedy-style mods that change an existing faction. They add a new faction instead. That runs into the same problems as question 1.

[b]Q: Where is [building]?[/b]
A: Some buildings cannot exist together. For example, Farmhouse and Efficient Farmhouse.

[b]Q: Why are there 2 of [building]?[/b]
A: When both versions can coexist and the stats are different, you get both (Mine and Efficient Mine, or Lumbermill and Industrial Lumbermill, or Industrial Large Pile).

[b]Q: Beavers are not breeding in Iron Teeth Barracks / houses![/b]
A: Use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3446296053]Configurable Housing[/url]. The game ties breeding to Folktails houses only. This mod cannot add it without also making Iron Teeth colonies breed.

[b]Q: The mod does not show up?[/b]
A: It only works with Timberborn v1.1+ (currently in Exp branch).
