[b]Global Inventory[/b] adds a colony-wide stock that is not stored in a building and not carried by beavers. [b]This mod does nothing on its own.[/b] It is a tool for other mods.

[img][/img]

An example of this mod in action is the [url]Power Overdrive[/url] mod.
