This mod helps modders to add new buildings to the game that can support other modded factions without knowing what they are. It is made to solve the problem of [b]"could you add this to [insert modded faction here]?"[/b].

Supported modded factions right now: Emberpelts, Leaf Coats, and Whitepaws. Contact me if you need any faction added.

This mod is designed to be optional, meaning players without this mod should not crash your mod, and they can still play with your buildings with Folktails and IronTeeth.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
Create your TemplateCollectionSpec files as usual for Folktails and Iron Teeth. For example, [b]TemplateCollection.MyModdedBuilding.Folktails.blueprint.json[/b]:
[code]
{
  "TemplateCollectionSpec": {
    "CollectionId": "Buildings.Folktails",
    "Blueprints": [
      "Buildings/Category/MyModdedBuildings/MyModdedBuildings.Folktails.blueprint"
    ]
  }
}
[/code]

Reuse that Folktails (or Iron Teeth) blueprint on the collection [b]Buildings.Support.ModdedFactions[/b]:
[code]
{
  "TemplateCollectionSpec": {
    "CollectionId": "Buildings.Support.ModdedFactions",
    "Blueprints": [
      "Buildings/Category/MyModdedBuildings/MyModdedBuildings.Folktails.blueprint"
    ]
  }
}
[/code]

That's it, your building then shows up for every supported modded faction. In your mod description, ask players to subscribe to this mod to use the buildings for modded factions.

There is also [b]Buildings.Support.AllFactions[/b], which includes Folktails and Iron Teeth as well. Use that only if you know how: buildings in that collection will not show up unless the player has this mod, and your models need all the materials those factions use.
