[b]More Building Renovations[/b] expands [b]Building Renovations[/b] with many more upgrades across production, agriculture, science, and logistics.

[h1]What you get[/h1]
This pack adds renovations in several groups:

[list]
[*] [b]Productions[/b]: Working-speed tiers for wood industry (Lumbermills, Gear Workshops, Wood Workshops), plus science-focused upgrades for Inventors, Observatories, and Numbercrunchers.
[*] [b]Agriculture[/b]: Faster planting and harvesting (timed buffs, with stronger tiers that replace weaker ones), and a permanent Growth Calendar so idle workers help nearby plants grow faster.
[*] [b]Logistics[/b]: Stronger haulers and builders: extra carrying capacity and movement speed on Hauling Posts, District Centers, and Builders' Huts.
[/list]

[list]
[*] [b]Heartwood Edge → Steadyframe → Harmony Join[/b]: permanent working-speed upgrades for wood workshops, unlocked in tiers.
[*] [b]Catalyst Infusion[/b]: temporary extra Science from Inventors (repeatable after it expires).
[*] [b]Lunar Ledger[/b]: Observatories generate Science at night without workers or power.
[*] [b]Deepcool Core[/b]: Numbercrunchers produce extra Science while fully submerged.
[*] [b]Growth Calendar[/b]: farmhouses, foresters, lumberjacks, gatherers, and tappers boost plant growth while a worker is idle.
[*] [b]Wooden / Ironseed Dibbler[/b] and [b]Wooden Flail / Ironleaf Scythe[/b]: short- and long-term planting and harvest speed boosts.
[*] [b]Fulcrum Rig[/b]: haulers and builders move more goods, faster.
[/list]
