Adds [b]Power Overdrivers[/b], a global science item you can slot onto powered manufactories so they work faster but draw a lot more power.

It is also a demonstration for my mod tools [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3806792686]Global Inventory[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3806793618]Science Shop[/url]. Please see how to use the Science Shop at its mod page.

[h1]Powered workshops[/h1]
After you have crafted at least one Overdriver, any [b]Manufactory[/b] that uses power gets a [b]Power Overdrive[/b] panel.
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15271754405426037975/9AC2D010B3EF30C1122C2B4619D5254294E5F496/?imw=5000&imh=5000&ima=fit&impolicy=Letterbox&imcolor=%23000000&letterbox=false[/img]

Click a level to equip it. That spends one Overdriver of that level and one of every level below it. Click the current level again to drop one step and refund that Overdriver.
[table]
[tr][th]Level[/th][th]Work speed[/th][th]Power usage[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]1[/td][td]+50%[/td][td]+75%[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]2[/td][td]+100%[/td][td]+150%[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]3[/td][td]+150%[/td][td]+225%[/td][/tr]
[/table]

It also applies to workerless buildings such as Iron Teeth's Numbercruncher, as long as the building uses power and runs a recipe.

[h1]Recipes[/h1]
Crafted at the Science Shop:
[table]
[tr][th]Output[/th][th]Science[/th][th]Goods[/th][th]Time[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]1 Basic Overdriver[/td][td]150[/td][td]50 Logs, 50 Water[/td][td]15 hours[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]1 Superior Overdriver[/td][td]250[/td][td]40 Planks, 40 Gears[/td][td]30 hours[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]1 Ultimate Overdriver[/td][td]500[/td][td]30 Treated Planks, 30 Metal Blocks[/td][td]30 hours[/td][/tr]
[/table]

Overdrivers live in global inventory, so they do not need a stockpile. You can equip and unequip them on any manufactory on the map with no hauling.

[h2]Conversion recipes[/h2]
The higher recipes can instead turn their ingredients into extra lower-tier Overdrivers:
