Adds a [b]Science Shop[/b]: a 1-worker workshop that spends Science Points per recipe cycle. Other mods add the recipes.

Selecting a recipe does not start work. Use the extra panel to run [b]1[/b], [b]2[/b], … cycles or [b]Unlimited[/b], then [b]Start[/b]. The shop stalls without enough science and subtracts the cost when a cycle actually begins.

[h1]Science Shop[/h1]
Last building in the [b]Science[/b] group.
[i]The paper stopped spinning. The gears did not.[/i]

- Unlock: 150 Science (bots: +1500 Science).
- Cost: 25 Planks, 25 Gears.
- Workers: 1.
- Recipes: none in this mod. Dependent mods add them.

[h1]Extending this mod[/h1]
Stack [b]ConsumeScienceRecipeSpec[/b] on a [b]RecipeSpec[/b] ([b]ScienceCost[/b] > 0) and include that recipe on a building that has empty [b]ConsumeScienceManufactorySpec[/b] (this shop already has it).
