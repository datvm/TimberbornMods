Adds a [b]Science Shop[/b] — a workshop that spends Science Points as a recipe cost.

[b]This mod ships no recipes.[/b] It is a tool for other mods. It needs [b]Global Inventory[/b] so some recipes can spend or produce global items.

See it used in [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3806793967]Power Overdrive[/url].

[h1]Science Shop[/h1]
Last building in the [b]Science[/b] group.
[i]The ink-stained and the grease-stained finally shook paws.[/i]
- Unlock: 150 Science (bots: 1500 Science).
- Cost: 25 Planks, 25 Gears.
- Workers: 1.

[h1]The Production Queue[/h1]
A normal workshop loops the selected recipe forever. Science is paid at the start of a cycle, so that is a bad idea here. The Shop has a queue instead: you enqueue only the cycles you want.

[b]Current production[/b] is the cycle already running. [b]Current queue[/b] is what starts after it. Pick a recipe in the dropdown and the details will show under it. [b]+[/b] queues one cycle. [b]−[/b] drops one. [b]Clear[/b] empties the rest of the queue. [b]Indefinitely[/b] loops that recipe until you turn it off.

A cycle that already started is not cancelled. Science and goods for that cycle are already spent. If the next cycle is waiting and you do not have enough Science, the Shop shows the usual science status icon, same as a workshop missing goods.
