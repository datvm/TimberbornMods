v11.3.2: Added support for modded factions (Emberpelts, Leaf Coat and Whitepaws). You need to subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3815025887]Modded Factions Building Support[/url]. For EP, you may need to subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3591896694]Treated Planks For Emberpelts[/url]. It is not tested for modded factions, however, and support is considered on a case-by-case basis.

v11.3.0:
- Added Horizontal and Vertical T Junctions. Thanks to the contribution of idmytro.

- Some simulation optimization.
Bug fixes:
- Valve Extract/Fill in does not work when a building is built after a Valve.
